Do you work with agencies?

Yes, as long as I am talking to whoever makes the decisions. Design by relay is where the good bits fall out.

Can you do just the design and we'll build it?

Sometimes. It works when your developers want a real spec and a design system rather than a picture of a website. It does not work when the design gets reinterpreted halfway through.

Do you take on artwork on its own?

Yes. Illustration, covers, and visual work that is not attached to a site or an app are all fair game — same four phases, different deliverables.

Will I be able to edit it myself?

Yes, in the places you will actually edit. I would rather give you three things you can confidently change than thirty you are frightened of.
