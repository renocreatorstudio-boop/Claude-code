The studio

One person, start to finish, no handoffs.

RenoCreatorStudio designs websites, apps and original artwork. Everything happens over the internet, which means where you are has never once been the problem.

Why one person

The handoff is where things go wrong

Most work gets worse at the seams — the strategist hands to the designer, the designer hands to the developer, and the thing that made it good in the first place quietly falls out somewhere in the middle.

Doing all three myself means the decision made in week one is still intact in week five, because the person who made it is the person shipping it.

What I believe

Four opinions, held firmly

Speed is a design decision

Not something you optimise afterwards. What a page weighs is decided when you choose what goes on it.

Accessibility is not a phase

It is how the thing is built from the first line, not an audit you fail near the end.

Motion has to mean something

Every animation either tells you where you are or it goes. Decoration that moves is just noise with extra steps.

You should be able to leave

You get the source, the files and no lock-in. Work that holds you hostage was never confident in the first place.

How it is priced

Fixed, before work starts

Every project is quoted as a fixed price against an agreed scope, so you are never watching a clock. If the work turns out larger than I estimated, that is my error and my cost.
