Why one person

The handoff is where sites go wrong

In most studios a strategist writes a deck, a designer interprets it, and a developer interprets that. Everyone does competent work. The thing that arrives at the end is nobody's argument.

I keep the whole chain in one head. When the build reveals that a section does not work — and it always does, around week four — the person who can change the design is already the person holding the code. That is the entire operating advantage, and it is why I will not scale past one.

What I believe

Four opinions, held firmly

Speed is a design decision

A page that takes four seconds has already told your visitor something about how you run your business. Every project has a performance budget agreed before a pixel is drawn, measured on a throttled connection.

Accessibility is not a phase

It is tested during the build, on real assistive technology, by a person. Automated tools catch about a third of what matters and none of what is embarrassing.

Motion has to mean something

Animation earns its place by explaining structure — where a thing came from, what belongs to what, that a press was received. Motion that only decorates is a cost with no return, and it is the first thing I cut.

You should be able to leave

No proprietary platform, no licence you have to keep paying me for, no lock-in of any kind. You get the source. If you hire someone else next year, they will be able to read it.

How it is priced

Fixed, before week two

Discovery is priced separately and small. At the end of it you get a fixed price for the whole project and a dated schedule. If the work turns out to be larger than I estimated, that is my error and my cost, not a change order.
