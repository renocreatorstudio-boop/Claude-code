Two-person knife workshop · 2024

The product page answers the email you were about to send.

Two people making kitchen knives by hand, drowning in the same six questions. Every unanswered question on a product page became an email, and every email was twenty minutes not spent at the forge.

01

The problem

Their catalogue was eleven knives that differ in ways a cook cares about and a website could not express: steel, hardness, edge geometry, handle length, how it will look in two years if you do nothing to it. The old pages had a photograph, a price, and a paragraph of romance.

So people asked. Six questions, over and over, in an inbox two makers checked between grinds. Some asked. Most just left, and the ones who guessed wrong sent the knife back.

02

What we did

I read eight months of their support inbox and counted. Six questions covered 71% of it. Those six became the spine of the product page — not an FAQ at the bottom, but the actual structure, in the order people ask them.

The sizing question needed something a paragraph cannot do: a hand-scale comparison you can hold up against your own. It is a drawn SVG, not a photo, so it is honest about proportion and weighs nothing.

  • Six-question page structure derived from the real support inbox
  • Scale comparison drawn to true size against a standard chef's knife
  • Steel and hardness explained in cooking terms, not metallurgy
  • Photography direction: the same knife at one year, three, and ten

03

How it was built

A hosted store backend with the entire front end rebuilt by hand. Product data comes from the platform; the rendering does not. That let the pages stay under 90KB with photography, on a platform whose default theme starts at ten times that.

The patina series was the hardest and most valuable shoot: the same blade, used and unpolished, at three ages. It removed the single biggest source of “is this a defect” emails.

04

What happened

Support email per hundred orders halved. Returns fell by a third. The care kit — always in the catalogue, never visible — now attaches to a quarter of orders.

We got our Tuesdays back. That is what the site did. It answers people better than we did over email, and faster.Joss Merrin, co-founder
