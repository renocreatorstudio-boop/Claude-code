One project at a time, so the answer is either a real yes or a fast no. You'll hear back within two working days, from me — not from a scheduling link.

What happens after you send it.

Within two working days you get a real reply from me — either a yes with a first question, or a no with the name of someone better suited.

If it is a yes, we spend forty minutes on a call working out whether the thing you think is the problem is the problem. That call is free and occasionally ends the conversation, which is a good outcome.

Then discovery — a week, priced small and separately — at the end of which you have a fixed price and a dated schedule, or a written note explaining why you should not spend the money at all.

What you get: a site that loads in under a second, says the true thing about your business on the first screen, and behaves well enough that people trust it before they've read a word.

Before you ask

How much does a site cost?

Between £6k and £25k for almost everything, depending on how many pages have to think. The price is fixed at the end of discovery, before design starts, and it does not move unless you change the scope.

How soon could you start?

Two slots are open for Q4. Discovery usually starts two to three weeks after the first call, and the whole project runs four to six weeks from there.

Do you work with agencies?

Occasionally, as a white-label design and build partner, when the agency is happy for me to talk to the client directly. Design by relay is the one thing this studio is built to avoid.

Can you just do the design and we'll build it?

Yes, though it is rarely the cheaper option. If your team builds it, I stay through the build to review and correct — otherwise you have bought pictures of a website.

Will I be able to edit it myself?

Wherever you will genuinely want to — a menu, a project list, an essay. I do not put a CMS behind pages that change once a year; that is complexity you pay for twice.
